What are colspan and rowspan?

colspan and rowspan are HTML table attributes used to merge table cells.

colspan merges columns (left to right).
rowspan merges rows (top to bottom).

<table border="1">
    <tr>
        <td colspan="2">Full Name</td>
        <td>Age</td>
    </tr>

    <tr>
        <td>Rahul</td>
        <td>Kumar</td>
        <td>20</td>
    </tr>
</table>

<table border="1">
    <tr>
        <th>Name</th>
        <th>Subject</th>
    </tr>

    <tr>
        <td rowspan="2">Rahul</td>
        <td>Math</td>
    </tr>

    <tr>
        <td>Science</td>
    </tr>
</table>

<table border="1">
    <tr>
        <th>Country</th>
        <th>City</th>
    </tr>

    <tr>
        <td rowspan="3">India</td>
        <td>Delhi</td>
    </tr>

    <tr>
        <td>Mumbai</td>
    </tr>

    <tr>
        <td>Chennai</td>
    </tr>
</table>
